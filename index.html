About the Book

What is Two Masters: Lucifer Wages War?

A powerful exploration of who we are, how we became who we are, and who we desire to become. Examining the social, religious, and political forces that shape us and keep us bound. A journey toward understanding the cost of freedom and the courage required to claim it.

About the Author

Ronald Harris is a minister of the gospel and a writer of biblical issues facing the world today he began his ministry about 50 years ago and has continue delivering the gospel message up to this day writing, preaching, teaching, and guiding people to live their lives according to God’s kingdom plan.
