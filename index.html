About the Book

What is Two Masters: Lucifer Wages War?

A powerful exploration of who we are, how we became who we are, and who we desire to become. Examining the social, religious, and political forces that shape us and keep us bound. A journey toward understanding the cost of freedom and the courage required to claim it.

About the Author

Ronald Harris is a passionate writer and storyteller, dedicated to exploring the complexities of human nature and the eternal struggle between good and evil. With a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the human psyche, Ronald crafts narratives that captivate readers and leave a lasting impact.
