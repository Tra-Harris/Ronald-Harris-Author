About Ronald Harris

Ronald Harris AKA Chief Longhair is a minister of the gospel and a writer of biblical issues facing the world today he began his ministry about 50 years ago and has continue delivering the gospel message up to this day writing, preaching, teaching, and guiding people to live their lives according to God’s kingdom plan.
