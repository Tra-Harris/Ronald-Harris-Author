About Ronald Harris

Ronald Harris AKA Chief longhair is a Bondservant to the King, an ambassador for the kingdom of heaven, a preacher and a child of God. he was increasing in the knowledge of Gods word, and zealous Oh how eager to spread the news as he saw it at that time. He had some great inspiring ministers to guide his learning, he put his commentary too good use, participated in several bible study courses, prison ministries, he sang in a gospel group evangelising while filling the office of an evangelist, doing personal evangelism, and street ministries, I could go on, But his studies led him to a different understanding of 2 Corinthians 6:17, coming from among the world to be separate took on a whole new meaning, he began to search for answers according to 2 Timothy 2:15 which says “study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman that need not to be ashamed rightly dividing the word of truth”. He began a personal evangelist program attempting to inform believers and nonbelievers of the existence of these two kingdoms / governments, and what separation truly means for the body of Christ, But we don’t want to know, and we can’t follow simple instructions so that we can get an understanding.
